The Vulnerabilities of Public SaaS

When your project management tool lives on someone else's server, you're exposed.

Data Exposure

Third-party servers store your confidential project data, trade secrets, and client information — a single breach away from exposure.

Price Hostage

Unexpected price hikes, tier changes, or feature paywalls — with no alternative because your entire workflow is locked into their ecosystem.

Attack Surface

Public-facing login pages invite brute force attacks, credential stuffing, web scrapers, and zero-day exploits targeting known platforms.

The Invisible Kanban Board

Tulay-Kanban is a visual project management board with modern organization — that strictly requires VPN access to even reach the login screen. No public URL. No exposure.
